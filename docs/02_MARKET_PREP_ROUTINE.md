# 🌅 Daily Market Preparation Routine

> The 20-minute morning routine that separates profitable traders from gamblers.

**Timing:** Do this at **11:30 AM IST** (1 hour before London open).
**Outcome:** A clear bias (bullish/bearish/no-trade) and 2–3 levels to watch.

---

## ⏰ The Routine (step-by-step checklist)

### ✅ Step 1 — Check the Economic Calendar (5 min)

**Go to:** [forexfactory.com/calendar](https://www.forexfactory.com/calendar) → set timezone to IST.

Look at **TODAY** + filter for:
- 🇺🇸 USD (most important for Gold)
- 🇪🇺 EUR
- 🇬🇧 GBP

**Color codes:**
- 🔴 **RED** = High impact → DO NOT trade 30 min before/after
- 🟠 **ORANGE** = Medium impact → tighten SL, be cautious
- 🟡 **YELLOW** = Low impact → ignore

**Decision tree:**

| What you see | Action |
|--------------|--------|
| 🔴 NFP / CPI / FOMC at 6 PM IST | Trade only 12:30–4:30 PM IST, close all by 5:30 PM |
| 🔴 Multiple red news | Skip the day. Protect capital. |
| 🟠 Only orange news | Normal trading, reduce size to 0.3% risk |
| Nothing red/orange | Full normal trading |

---

### ✅ Step 2 — Check Macro Bias (5 min)

Three quick checks:

#### A) DXY (US Dollar Index)
**Open chart:** `TVC:DXY` on TradingView, daily timeframe.
- DXY trending UP → Gold likely DOWN (look for shorts)
- DXY trending DOWN → Gold likely UP (look for longs)
- DXY ranging → either side OK

#### B) US 10-Year Yield
**Open chart:** `TVC:US10Y`, daily.
- Yields UP → Gold pressure DOWN
- Yields DOWN → Gold pressure UP

#### C) Geopolitics / Risk Sentiment
**Read 2 headlines on:**
- [reuters.com/markets](https://www.reuters.com/markets/)
- [investing.com/news](https://www.investing.com/news/)

Looking for: war escalation, banking issues, China data, oil spikes.
- Risk-OFF (fear) → Gold UP
- Risk-ON (calm) → Gold DOWN (usually)

**Bias output:** Write one line in your journal: *"DXY up, yields up, no crisis → bias SHORT gold today"*

---

### ✅ Step 3 — Mark Key Levels on XAUUSD Chart (5 min)

Open **XAUUSD M15 + Daily** charts.

**On Daily chart, mark with horizontal lines:**
1. **PDH** (Previous Day High)
2. **PDL** (Previous Day Low)
3. **PDC** (Previous Day Close)
4. **Weekly High & Low** (this week so far)
5. **Last major swing high/low** (visible on Daily)
6. **Round numbers** near current price (2000, 2050, 2100…)

**On M15 chart, your CPR script already shows:**
- Pivot, TC, BC ✅
- Asian High/Low (after 12:30 IST) ✅

> 💡 Save these as a **TradingView template** — apply with one click each day.

---

### ✅ Step 4 — Asian Range Analysis (3 min)

After 12:00 IST, look at the Asian session (already finished):
- **Range size:** Compare today's Asian range to last 5 days.
  - Smaller than average → BIG breakout likely 🔥
  - Larger than average → choppy day, smaller moves expected
- **Position relative to PDH/PDL:**
  - Asian range INSIDE prior day = compression → breakout
  - Asian range broke PDH = bullish bias confirmed

---

### ✅ Step 5 — Pre-Trade Checklist (2 min)

Before 12:30 IST, ask yourself:

- [ ] Am I mentally calm? (No fight at home, slept well?)
- [ ] Is there red news in next 6 hours?
- [ ] Is my bias clear (long/short/no-trade)?
- [ ] Are my key levels marked?
- [ ] Is my position size calculated?
- [ ] Is my SL placed at a logical level (not random)?

If **all yes** → you're ready. If **any no** → fix it or skip.
