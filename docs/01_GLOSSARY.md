# 📖 Trading Glossary — Every Term Explained Simply

> Reference dictionary. Bookmark this. Read it once fully, then come back when confused.

## 🧠 SMART MONEY CONCEPTS (SMC)

**Smart Money** = Banks, hedge funds, market makers. They move ~80% of forex volume.
**Retail traders** = You and me (~20%). We get hunted.

The whole idea of SMC: **track where the big players are operating, trade WITH them.**

---

### 1. Liquidity

**Definition:** Areas where many stop-loss orders or pending orders are sitting → easy money for big players to fill their orders.

**Where liquidity sits:**
- **Above recent swing highs** → buy stops (people who went short put SL above)
- **Below recent swing lows** → sell stops (people who went long put SL below)
- **Round numbers** (2000, 2050, 2100 on Gold) → psychological levels

**Example:**
> XAUUSD swing high at 2050. Everyone shorted at 2048 with SL at 2052. There are 1000s of buy-stop orders at 2052. A bank wanting to SELL 10,000 oz needs buyers — so they PUSH price up to 2052 → triggers all buy stops → bank sells INTO that liquidity → price reverses down.

---

### 2. Liquidity Sweep (a.k.a. Stop Hunt / Liquidity Grab)

**Definition:** Price quickly spikes through a key level (high/low), hits stops, then reverses.

**Visual:** A long wick poking through a swing high, then closing back below.

**Why it matters:** This is the **#1 reversal signal** in SMC. After a sweep, expect the opposite direction.

**Example on chart:**
```
       /\  ← sweep (wick above high)
      /  \
─────●────●───── ← prior swing high
    /      \
   /        \   ← reverses down
```

---

### 3. Fair Value Gap (FVG) / Imbalance

**Definition:** A 3-candle pattern where there's a price gap between candle 1 and candle 3.

**Bullish FVG:** Low of candle 3 is HIGHER than high of candle 1.
**Bearish FVG:** High of candle 3 is LOWER than low of candle 1.

**Why it matters:** Markets like efficiency. Price often returns to "fill" these gaps before continuing.

**Visual (bullish FVG):**
```
Candle 1:  high = 2000
Candle 2:  big bullish bar  ← imbalance candle
Candle 3:  low  = 2010      ← GAP between 2000-2010
```
That zone (2000–2010) is the FVG. Price will likely retest it later → great entry zone.

---

### 4. Order Block (OB)

**Definition:** The last bearish candle before a strong bullish move (bullish OB), or last bullish candle before a strong bearish move (bearish OB). These are zones where institutions placed big orders.

**Use:** Mark these zones, when price returns to them → enter trade in the direction of the original move.

---

### 5. Break of Structure (BoS) & Change of Character (CHoCH)

- **BoS:** Trend continues — price breaks the prior swing high (uptrend) or low (downtrend).
- **CHoCH (Change of Character):** Trend reverses — price breaks the prior swing low DURING an uptrend (warning sign).

**Simple rule:**
- BoS = trend is still strong → trade with it
- CHoCH = trend may be ending → wait for confirmation

---

### 6. Premium & Discount

Imagine the recent range from low to high. Split it in half.
- **Above midpoint = Premium** (expensive) → look to SHORT
- **Below midpoint = Discount** (cheap) → look to LONG

## 📊 TRADITIONAL CONCEPTS

### 7. Support & Resistance
- **Support:** Price floor — buyers step in here
- **Resistance:** Price ceiling — sellers step in here
- When broken, they often **flip** (old resistance becomes new support)

### 8. CPR (Central Pivot Range) — *you know this*
- **Pivot** = (H+L+C)/3 of previous day
- **TC** = (Pivot - BC) + Pivot
- **BC** = (H+L)/2
- **Narrow CPR** (TC-BC small) = trending day expected ✅
- **Wide CPR** = sideways/choppy day, avoid

### 9. VWAP (Volume Weighted Average Price)
**Definition:** The average price weighted by volume, resets daily.

**Why it matters:** Big funds use VWAP to measure if they got a "good fill". They BUY below VWAP, SELL above. So:
- Price above VWAP + uptrend = institutions accumulating
- Pullback to VWAP = institutional buying zone → great long entry

### 10. EMA (Exponential Moving Average)
Faster-reacting average. Common settings:
- **EMA 20** → short-term trend
- **EMA 50** → medium trend
- **EMA 200** → long-term trend (the "trend line of trends")

**Rule:** Trade ONLY in the direction of EMA 200 on your timeframe.

### 11. ATR (Average True Range)
Measures volatility (avg candle size over 14 bars).
**Use:** Set SL = 1× to 1.5× ATR → adapts to market conditions automatically.

### 12. RSI (Relative Strength Index)
0–100 scale.
- **>70** = overbought (potential reversal down)
- **<30** = oversold (potential reversal up)
- **50** = neutral, momentum line

### 13. Pivot Points (Daily)
PDH (Previous Day High), PDL (Previous Day Low), PDC (Close).
Classic targets and reversal zones.

## 🕒 SESSION & TIME

### 14. Trading Sessions (IST)

| Session | IST Time | Behavior |
|---------|----------|----------|
| **Sydney** | 03:30 – 12:30 | Low volume |
| **Tokyo/Asian** | 05:00 – 14:00 | Quiet, ranges form |
| **London** | 12:30 – 21:30 | 🔥 Biggest moves, breakouts |
| **New York** | 18:00 – 02:30 | 🔥 News spikes, trends |
| **London+NY overlap** | 18:00 – 21:30 | 🔥🔥 Most volatile (best trading) |

### 15. Asian Range
The high-low of the Asian session. **London often breaks one side** of it → breakout trade setup.

## 💰 RISK MANAGEMENT

### 16. R / RR / Risk:Reward Ratio
- **1R** = your stop-loss distance in money
- If you risk $100 and target $300 → **3R trade** (1:3 RR)

### 17. Position Size Formula
$$ \text{Lots} = \frac{\text{Account} \times \text{Risk\%}}{\text{SL pips} \times \text{Pip value}} $$

Example: $10,000 acct, 0.5% risk = $50. SL = 20 pips on XAUUSD ($1/pip per 0.01 lot) → **2.5 micro lots**.

### 18. Drawdown (DD)
- **Daily DD:** Max loss from day's starting balance (FTMO: 5%)
- **Max DD:** Max loss from highest balance (FTMO: 10%)
- Breach = account loss

### 19. Funded Account / Prop Firm
You pay a fee → pass a challenge → trade firm's capital → keep 70–90% of profits.
Examples: **FTMO, MyForexFunds, FundedNext, The5ers**

## 📰 FUNDAMENTAL TERMS

### 20. Macro vs Micro
- **Macro:** Big-picture economy (interest rates, GDP, inflation, war, central banks)
- **Micro:** Specific to instrument (gold = mining output, dollar strength)

### 21. Hawkish vs Dovish (Central Banks)
- **Hawkish** 🦅 = Fed wants to RAISE rates → USD UP, Gold DOWN
- **Dovish** 🕊️ = Fed wants to CUT rates → USD DOWN, Gold UP

### 22. Key Reports (what moves Gold)

| Event | Frequency | Impact on Gold |
|-------|-----------|----------------|
| **NFP (Non-Farm Payrolls)** | 1st Friday/month | 🔴 Huge volatility |
| **CPI (Inflation)** | Monthly | 🔴 Huge — high CPI = USD up = gold down (usually) |
| **FOMC Rate Decision** | 8x/year | 🔴 Massive |
| **FOMC Minutes** | 3 weeks after FOMC | 🟡 Medium |
| **PPI** | Monthly | 🟡 Medium |
| **Unemployment Rate** | Monthly with NFP | 🟡 Medium |
| **PMI** | Monthly | 🟡 Medium |
| **Jobless Claims** | Weekly Thursday | 🟢 Small |
| **Retail Sales** | Monthly | 🟡 Medium |
| **Powell speeches** | Random | 🔴 Huge spikes |

### 23. Safe Haven
Asset people buy when scared. **Gold, JPY, CHF, USD, US Treasuries.**
Risk-off events (war, crisis) → safe havens UP.

### 24. Correlation
- **DXY (Dollar Index) UP → Gold DOWN** (usually inverse)
- **Real yields UP → Gold DOWN**
- **VIX UP → Gold UP** (fear = gold buy)

## 🛠️ EXECUTION TERMS

### 25. Spread
Difference between bid and ask price. Your "entry cost".
- XAUUSD typical spread: 15–30 cents (good broker)
- Widens during news → don't trade through news

### 26. Slippage
You wanted to enter at 2050.00, you got filled at 2050.50 = 50 cents slippage. Common on news.

### 27. Lot Sizes
- **Standard lot** = 100 oz gold ($1 = $1/cent move)
- **Mini lot** = 10 oz
- **Micro lot** = 1 oz (this is what 0.01 lot means)

### 28. Pip / Point on XAUUSD
On Gold, "1 pip" usually = $0.10 move. Some brokers call it $0.01. **Check your broker.**

### 29. Pending Orders
- **Buy Stop:** Order to buy ABOVE current price (for breakouts)
- **Sell Stop:** Order to sell BELOW current price (for breakouts)
- **Buy Limit:** Order to buy BELOW current price (for pullbacks)
- **Sell Limit:** Order to sell ABOVE current price (for pullbacks)

## 📈 CANDLE PATTERNS (quick)

| Pattern | Means |
|---------|-------|
| **Hammer** | Long lower wick → rejection of lows → bullish |
| **Shooting Star** | Long upper wick → rejection of highs → bearish |
| **Engulfing** | Body fully covers prior candle → strong reversal |
| **Doji** | Open ≈ Close → indecision → reversal possible |
| **Inside Bar** | Candle inside prior range → compression → breakout coming |
| **Pin Bar** | Long wick, small body → rejection at level |

## 🚨 RED FLAGS (avoid trading when)

- 30 min before/after **NFP, CPI, FOMC**
- During **Powell live speech**
- **First & last 5 min** of each session (extreme volatility)
- **Friday after 8 PM IST** (weekend gap risk)
- **Spread > 2× normal** (broker issue)
