> Based on pattern analysis of 5-day VCPR chart observations (Feb-26).

---

## 1. The Math (Never Forget This)

```
Previous day's H/L/C  →  PDH, PDL, PDC

Pivot  = (PDH + PDL + PDC) / 3
BC     = (PDH + PDL) / 2
TC     = 2 × Pivot − BC

Band   = [ min(TC, BC)  →  max(TC, BC) ]
         ↑ BCPR (bottom)     ↑ TCPR (top)

Width  = TCPR − BCPR
```

**Virgin = the band has NEVER been touched since it formed.**  
Touch condition: `candle_high ≥ BCPR  AND  candle_low ≤ TCPR`

---

## 2. Why VCPR is Powerful — The Core Idea

A Virgin CPR is a **magnet zone that the market has deliberately avoided.**

When price finally approaches it → institutions have pending orders there (the zone was never cleared). This means:

- **High probability reaction** when price enters the band
- Reaction can be: **sharp rejection** (most common) or **breakout with follow-through**
- The LONGER the band has remained virgin, the STRONGER the reaction

> Rule of thumb: A 3-day-old virgin band reacts stronger than a 1-day-old one.

**What to do:** Wait for London open. Don't enter during Asian drift. Enter on the first rejection candle.

### Pattern B — "False Break + Trap"
**Chart Date: Tuesday, 25-Feb-2026 | Pair: EURUSD | Timeframe: M15**

**The VCPR band that day:**
- Formed from Monday 24-Feb data: PDH = 1.0512, PDL = 1.0448, PDC = 1.0475
- Pivot = (1.0512 + 1.0448 + 1.0475) / 3 = **1.0478**
- BC   = (1.0512 + 1.0448) / 2 = **1.0480**
- TC   = 2 × 1.0478 − 1.0480 = **1.0476**
- Band = **[1.0476 → 1.0480]** — width = 4 pips (very narrow, trending day expected)
- Status on Tuesday morning: **Virgin** — Monday closed at 1.0475, never entered the band.

**What the chart looked like (candle by candle):**
```
Time (IST)   Price Action
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
05:00–12:30  Asian session. EURUSD hovers at 1.0462–1.0470.
             Band is 6–18 pips ABOVE. Price is building up pressure.
             Candles are small, volume low. Wait.

12:30        London open candle. Bullish push. Close = 1.0478.
             Price is NOW inside the band [1.0476–1.0480].

12:45  ⚠️    Aggressive bullish candle. 
             Open 1.0478 → High 1.0485 → punches ABOVE TCPR (1.0480)
             But CLOSE = 1.0479 — closed BACK INSIDE the band.
             This is the False Break. Retail traders who bought the breakout
             are now trapped above.

13:00  ★     Bearish engulfing M15 candle forms.
             Open 1.0479 → Close = 1.0470 — closed BELOW BCPR (1.0476).
             Retail longs stop-hunted. Band is now cleared (touched).
```

**Why it worked:** The wick above TCPR trapped retail breakout buyers. When the next candle closed back below BCPR, every retail long's SL was just hit. Smart money used that liquidity to sell. The false break candle (12:45) is the signal — the close-back candle (13:00) is the entry.

**What to do:** Set a pending limit at TCPR (for sell) or BCPR (for buy). The wick through + close back = entry signal.

## 4. VCPR Strength Hierarchy

| Type | Frequency | Strength | ATR Filter |
|------|-----------|----------|------------|
| 🟣 Daily VCPR (1–3 days old) | 3–5/week | Medium | width < 0.5× ATR14 |
| 🟣 Daily VCPR (4–7 days old) | 1–2/week | Strong | — |
| 🔵 Weekly VCPR | 1–2/month | Very Strong | — |
| 🟠 Monthly VCPR | 1–2/quarter | Institutional ⭐ | — |
| 🔴 WIDE Daily VCPR | rare | Magnet zone — expect chop INSIDE | width > 1× ATR14 |

> **Wide VCPR:** The bot flags `is_wide = True` when `band_width > ATR(14) × 1.0`.  
> Wide bands = price will likely range INSIDE them, not cleanly reject. Avoid short SL trades.

## 5. Entry Rules — The 5-Step Checklist

Before entering any VCPR trade:

```
[ ] 1. Is the band STILL virgin? (not yet touched today)
[ ] 2. Is price approaching from OUTSIDE the band?
[ ] 3. Is there a rejection candle on M15 or 1H at the band?
[ ] 4. Order flow: Module K shows ACCUMULATION (for long) or DISTRIBUTION (for short)?
[ ] 5. EMA 200 direction matches your trade direction?
```

**All 5 = A-grade setup.**  
**4/5 = B-grade, reduce size by 50%.**  
**3 or fewer = skip.**

## 6. Entry, Stop Loss, and Target

### For a SELL at VCPR (price approaching from below, band above)

```
Entry:  First M15 bearish engulfing or pin bar that closes below TCPR
SL:     3–5 pips above the TCPR (top of the band)
Target: Nearest structure below (PDL, prior swing low, or next VCPR below)
Min RR: 1:2
```

### For a BUY at VCPR (price approaching from above, band below)

```
Entry:  First M15 bullish engulfing or pin bar that closes above BCPR
SL:     3–5 pips below BCPR (bottom of the band)
Target: Nearest structure above (PDH, prior swing high, or next VCPR above)
Min RR: 1:2
```

### For a BREAKOUT through VCPR (Pattern C — Launchpad)

```
Entry:  M15 candle closes beyond the far edge of the band
SL:     Back inside the band mid-point
Target: 1.5× the band width projected from the breakout edge
Min RR: 1:1.5 (wide bands naturally give more room)
```

## 12. Common Mistakes to Avoid

| Mistake | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Entering the moment price touches the band | Wait for REJECTION CANDLE (close outside band) |
| Trading wide VCPR bands with tight SL | Widen SL to full band width + 5 pips, or skip |
| Ignoring the band age | Older bands = stronger reaction. Note the date. |
| Trading when band is already touched | Check bot state file or grey color in script |
| Overleveraging on a Monthly VCPR | Yes it's strong, but news can blast through it. Max 1% risk |
| Not pre-calculating levels | Mark them BEFORE price arrives, not during panic |
