## 📌 KEY EMOTIONAL HOOKS USED

**Audience 1 — Experienced loss traders (Nifty/BankNifty):**
1. **Pain hook** — Relate to their real loss experience (Thursday expiry, Theta)
2. **Enemy reveal** — "Ithu unoda தப்பு illa" — Theta is the system problem, not their intelligence
3. **Relief** — Forex has none of that

**Audience 2 — Complete beginners (want to start trading):**
4. **Confusion hook** — 10 strategies, no clarity, don't know where to start
5. **Simplicity promise** — One strategy, step by step, explained clearly in Tanglish
6. **Easy entry** — No complex jargon, taught like a friend explains

**Both audiences:**
7. **Accessibility** — ₹5,000 is enough (removes "I don't have money" objection)
8. **Low effort** — 30 minutes at lunch break (removes "I don't have time" objection)
9. **Scarcity** — Seats limited (urgency to book)
10. **Free gift** — Bot + cheat sheet + recording (increases perceived value beyond ₹4,999)
