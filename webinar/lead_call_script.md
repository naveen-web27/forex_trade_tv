# 📞 Lead Follow-Up Call Script — Webinar Conversion
**Use this for all 10 Google Form leads**
**Language:** Tanglish | **Goal:** Convert enquiry → paid registration

---

## STEP 1 — OPENING (First 30 seconds)
Don't sell immediately. Build comfort first.

> *"Vanakkam, [Name] sir/ma'am. Naan [your name] pesurein. Neenga forex webinar pathi enquiry pannirunteenga — athu pathi pesalaam nu call panninen. 2 minutes time irukaa?"*

- Yes → continue to Step 2
- Busy → *"Seri sir, enna time convenience-aa irukum? Naan call pannuren."* — fix a time, hang up politely.

---

## STEP 2 — QUALIFY FIRST (Ask, don't pitch yet)

**Q1:** *"Currently neenga trading pannureengalaa — illa start pannanum nu irukkeengalaa?"*

**Q2:** *"Nifty BankNifty options la experience irukaa — illa forex pudhusa?"*

**Q3:** *"Enna expect panreengaa ithu learn pannitu — side income venumaa, full time venumaa, illa funded account try pannanum-aa?"*

> Listen more than you talk. Their answers tell you which angle to use.

---

## STEP 3 — HANDLE THE 3 MAIN QUESTIONS

---

### ❓ "Forex trading enna — naanku theriyaathu"

> *"Nifty BankNifty options madiri than — aaana oru periya difference iruku. Options la time decay iruku — Thursday expiry varumbothu unoda option value zero aaguthu, direction correct-aa irunthalum. Forex la athu illai. Price move aagalayanna unoda trade value same-aa irukkum. Time unoda enemy illai athulayi. Namba oru simple method use pannuvom — price action — chart paathu price enga pogudhunu purinjukka varudhillai nu solveengaa, athu puriyara madiri step by step sollikudurom — Tamil la."*

---

### ❓ "Already candlestick theriyum — profit eduka mudiyumaa?"

> *"Super sir — candlestick therinjaa half work aagiduthu. Namba strategy ke oru key level use pannuvom — oru specific zone irukku, athula price first time vandhu react pannum. Athu exact-aa enna nu webinar la explain pannuvom — with real charts, real entries, real SL. Candlestick already therinjaa neenga pattern identify pannuvombothe faster-aa understand pannuveenga. Unakku actually more advantage than someone starting fresh."*

---

### ❓ "Neenga funded account pass pannirukkeengalaa — students profit eduthangalaa?"

> *"Aamaa sir — naan personally FTMO challenge attempt panninen. [Result sollunga — pass aanaa sollunga, illana honest-aa 'attempt panninen, athu experience than ithu teach panra confidence kuduththu' nu sollunga].*
>
> *Strategy-oda backtest results iruku — February 2026 la 5 days, 8 trades, 6 winners, average 2R. Numbers hide panna maaten.*
>
> *Students pathi — ithu first batch sir. Athanala 10 students seat limit vachchirkom. 30 days paper trade pannitten potthu daily check panna ready-aa irukein."*

**Key line to always say:**
> *"Naan unakku guarantee kudukka maaten — market guarantee kudukkathu. Aaana oru clear system, oru proper process — athu teach pannuvein. System follow pannaa edge irukku — numbers athu prove pannudu."*

---

## STEP 4 — PITCH (Customize based on their answer in Step 2)

| They said | You say |
|---|---|
| No experience | *"Ithu unakku perfect sir — zero experience iruntalum okay. Tamil la puriyara madiri explain pannuvom."* |
| Options background | *"Theta problem already feel panniruveenga. Forex la athu illai nu first hand understand pannuveenga."* |
| Want funded account | *"Namba exactly athu pathi oru full hour pesuvom — FTMO, FundedNext, which firm, risk rules — everything covered."* |

---

## STEP 5 — PRICE OBJECTION

If they say *"₹4,999 jaasthi"*:

> *"Understand panraen sir. Oru comparison solli parunga — Nifty la oru bad trade — 5,000 easily pogudhillai? Athu oru trade. Ithu — oru full system, 5 hours, real charts, free bot, cheat sheet, recording — athu ellam one time ₹4,999. Bot alone daily 15 minutes save pannudu — month la 7 hours. Unga time value enna?"*

---

## STEP 6 — CLOSE

> *"So sir, neenga interested-aa iruntaa — seats porathukala, 10 than iruku. Registration link unoda WhatsApp la anupuraen. Confirm panniteengaa?"*

If hesitating:
> *"Neenga decide pannitu call pannuveenga — problem illai. Aaana seat availability guarantee panna maaten. Link anupuraen — parunga, decide pannunga."*

---

## QUICK CHEAT SHEET (Keep near phone)

| Lead says | You say |
|---|---|
| "Forex theriyaathu" | Theta comparison → simple method → Tamil explanation |
| "Candlestick theriyum" | You have advantage → faster learning |
| "Proof irukaa?" | Backtest numbers → honest about students → system over guarantee |
| "Costly" | One bad Nifty trade vs full system |
| "Think pannuven" | Send link → seats limited → no pressure but urgency |
| "Already trading" | Ask which market → position accordingly |

---

> **Golden rule:** Ask more, talk less for the first 2 minutes.
> You are not selling a webinar — you are solving their specific problem.
